# Marketing strategy

Live product = chamber on `main`. Vision = [PRODUCT & DISCOVERY PLAN](https://app.notion.com/p/3ccf4949ea1481728918eca26fa21637) only.

## Positioning

**CiteBench** = legal practice assistant for Indian lawyers (diary + matters + inbox + research).

**Not:** an AI that answers legal questions · a case-law search engine alone · a CRM · a generic doc generator · the GoI Nyaya Setu chatbot or police dashboard.

## Audiences

1. Practising advocates (primary)
2. Senior advocates / chamber leads (trust + juniors)
3. Juniors doing research
4. Builder / build-in-public peers
5. Press / rename clarifiers

## Funnel

Awareness (X, WhatsApp, `/story`) → Trust (citation gate, human confirm) → Try (sample chamber) → Feedback (lawyer asks) → Paid chamber (when card live — **not yet**)

## Cadence (starter)

| Cadence | Channel | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| 2–3× / week | X | Prefer CONFIRMED |
| Ongoing | WhatsApp status when shipping | CONFIRMED |
| Continuous | Replies to rename / AI-lawyer confusions | CONFIRMED |
| Weekly | 5–10 personal DMs / outreach emails | CONFIRMED |
| Rare | VISION tease | Must say *building toward* |

## Campaigns

1. **Rename** — hygiene vs GoI brand
2. **Trust rules** — verified = retrieved
3. **Chamber launch** — five surfaces
4. **Lawyer discovery** — feedback asks
5. **Build in public** — CLAIM-labeled phone story
6. **Vision tease** — only after live is clear
7. **Outreach** — email/DM sequences

## Vision beats {#vision-beats}

Do **not** ship as live features:

1. Order → action pipeline
2. Hearing brief as default pre-court workflow
3. Morning command centre (“what do I need to do today?”)
4. Proceeding state machine (not a checklist)
5. Unified lawyer inbox

## Success signals (without inventing numbers)

- Qualitative lawyer replies on “what would you not trust”
- Sample chamber usage stories personally witnessed
- Zero public posts that confuse CiteBench with GoI Nyaya Setu
- Leave `[need number]` blank until measured
