# Format guides

Use with the Notion Content Calendar. Full paste text: [paste-bank.md](paste-bank.md).

## X / Twitter

- ≤280 characters per post; use threads for trust rules
- Lead with product truth, not stopwatch
- End CONFIRMED product posts with **Not legal advice** when making product claims
- Photo: `public/og.jpg` — no fake memo screenshots

## Article

- Canonical longform is `/story` — share it; don’t fork a conflicting origin myth
- Future articles: one job per piece; cite claim level in the standfirst if VISION

## Replies

- Correct rename / AI-lawyer / speed myths with short banks
- Don’t dunk on GoI products; distinguish calmly

## Marketing

- Pitch blurbs for bios, landing, LinkedIn
- Always: sample free ≠ trial started; card not live

## WhatsApp status

- Short beats; use `public/og.jpg`
- No fake memo screenshots

## Personal messages

- Personalized with court/city when possible
- Ask a trust question; offer sample chamber
- Not mass-spam templates without context

## Outreach emails

- Subject names the distinction or the ask
- Body: what it is / what it isn’t / trust rule / CTA
- No invented metrics; no “official” language

## Claim checklist before Approve

- [ ] Channel correct
- [ ] Claim level honest
- [ ] No `[need number]` filled with guesses
- [ ] Vision labeled if used
- [ ] Vineet yes before Voice posts
